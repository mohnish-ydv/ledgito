# Ledgito 1.1 — Full Suite Release

## Release objective

This release implements the four product phases requested for the offline-first app: UX perfection, planning/money management, wealth and analytics. It builds on the existing ledger engine rather than adding disconnected demo screens.

## Product flow

Home now exposes Planning and Wealth entry points. More contains the complete tool catalogue. Planning centre aggregates commitments, outstanding balances, goals and a 90-day agenda. Wealth centre aggregates net worth, holdings, liabilities, allocation and recorded performance. Insights turns cleared ledger data and active budgets into transparent, local calculations.

## Planning systems

Bills, EMIs, loans, debt, liabilities and goals use workspace activity events. Payments cannot exceed the remaining balance. Where an account and expense category are selected, a payment can be atomically posted as a cleared ledger transaction. EMI/loan/debt/liability due dates advance monthly after a recorded payment while the item remains active.

Planned payments, bills and scheduled repayments participate in the 90-day forecast. Subscriptions support weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly billing cycles and repeat across the forecast and planning agenda. Recurring rules honour their end date and occurrence limit. The calendar shows transactions plus planning items due on the selected day.

## Wealth systems

Investments, mutual funds, gold, fixed deposits, PPF, EPF, crypto and assets support manual value-history updates. Net worth combines included accounts, current holdings/assets and outstanding liabilities in the base currency. Recorded performance compares current values with user-entered cost basis. No external price data is implied.

## Analytics systems

The reporting engine calculates savings rate, spending change against the previous equal-length period, budget utilisation, over-budget count, category share, weekday concentration and a private financial-planning score. The score is a deterministic bookkeeping indicator—not a credit score—and the UI states this directly.

## UX and accessibility

The Quick Add FAB is dock-integrated and restored on root screens. Date fields use pickers. System bars and display cut-outs are handled. Custom charts expose accessibility descriptions. Empty states, hidden-amount behaviour and navigation into planning/wealth tools are implemented throughout.

## Compatibility and deliberate exclusions

- Package ID: `com.mohnishraj.goldmineledger`
- Room database: v3, unchanged
- Older database upgrades: explicit migrations retained
- Older portable backups: retained through the legacy internal format identifier
- No internet permission
- No OAuth, Supabase, Google Drive, bank sync or live-market feed in this milestone
- No claim of financial, investment or credit advice
