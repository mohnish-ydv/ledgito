# Audience and Jobs to Be Done

## Primary users

1. Students and first-time budgeters who need guided setup.
2. Salaried users tracking cash, bank accounts, wallets and recurring bills.
3. Freelancers and gig workers separating income and expenses.
4. Households that want reliable records without handing data to a finance service.

## Secondary users

- Travellers using multiple currencies manually
- Users migrating from discontinued or paywalled trackers
- Privacy-conscious users who avoid mandatory accounts

## Core jobs

- “When I spend or receive money, help me record it quickly so my balance stays trustworthy.”
- “When money moves between my own accounts, record it without treating it as income or expense.”
- “When bills repeat, prepare or record them reliably without duplicate entries.”
- “When I review my finances, show any period I choose without locking basic history behind payment.”
- “When I change phones or uninstall the app, let me restore everything—including receipts.”
- “When I start budgeting, guide me instead of assuming I already understand the setup.”

## User anxieties the app must remove

- Losing years of records
- Export failing when needed most
- Updates removing important functions
- Hidden subscription limits
- Wrong balances after transfers
- Recurring entries duplicating
- Budgets breaking after the first month
