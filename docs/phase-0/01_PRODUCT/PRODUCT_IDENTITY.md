# Product Identity

## Brand architecture

- Portfolio: Goldmine
- Product: Goldmine Ledger
- Descriptor: Offline Expense & Budget Manager
- Developer credit: Mohnish Raj
- Package: `com.mohnishraj.goldmineledger`

## Brand promise

**Private by default. Useful without internet. Easy to leave.**

## Product personality

- Calm, not gamified
- Clear, not crowded
- Respectful, not manipulative
- Precise, not intimidating
- Helpful, not judgmental

## Primary message

Track income, expenses, transfers, budgets and receipts without surrendering control of your data.

## Logo direction

A compact open-ledger shape formed by two balanced panels, with a subtle downward-to-level line representing spending brought under control. Avoid coins, rupee symbols, piggy banks, bank buildings and crypto imagery so the brand remains global and trustworthy.

## Voice

Use plain action language:
- “Add expense” instead of “Create debit transaction”
- “Move money” instead of “Initiate transfer”
- “Backup complete” instead of “Data synchronisation succeeded”

Never shame the user for spending. Insights describe patterns, not morality.
