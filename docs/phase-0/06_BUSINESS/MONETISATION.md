# Monetisation

## Principle

Money is earned from optional convenience and recurring-cost services, not by making the user’s own records unusable.

## Free forever

- Unlimited local accounts and transactions
- Income, expense and transfer
- Categories, tags and search
- Recurring transactions
- Monthly/weekly/yearly/custom budgets
- Day through all-time reports
- Six-month/year/custom views
- Receipts and attachments
- CSV/JSON export
- Complete backup and restore
- Encrypted backup
- App lock

## Lifetime Pro — planned

Candidate launch structure, subject to later store/pricing review:
- Early supporter: ₹149 one-time
- Standard: ₹299 one-time

Pro value:
- Advanced dashboard layouts
- Forecasting and deeper comparisons
- Extra widgets
- Premium report templates
- Extra visual themes
- Bulk-edit conveniences

The app must remain genuinely useful without Pro.

## Optional future subscription

Only after real recurring infrastructure exists:
- Multi-device real-time sync
- Shared household ledger
- Managed cloud backup history

Users who do not subscribe keep the full offline core.

## Ads

- No forced interstitials
- No banner on Home, transaction editor, budget, backup or restore
- No credit refill required to use core features
- Optional rewarded ad may only unlock a temporary cosmetic or Pro-preview item and must never interrupt a workflow
- Initial v1 should ship without ads SDK until retention and trust are measured
