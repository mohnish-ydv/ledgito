# Ledgito 1.2 — Professional Experience

This release turns the full-suite finance foundation into a more focused, responsive and personal daily experience while preserving the Room v3 database and the offline-only privacy model.

## 1. Professional UX

- The centre Quick Add control is now physically constrained inside the bottom dock, brought above OEM elevation layers after layout, and never relies on a fragile translation.
- Quick Add opens a Material bottom sheet with Expense, Income, Transfer, Bill, Goal and Investment actions.
- Home can be customised locally: Monthly pulse, Budget pulse, Planning & wealth, Recent activity and Planned money can be shown or hidden.
- Screen entrance motion, haptic confirmation, accessible chart descriptions and Material bottom sheets improve feedback without adding an animation dependency.
- Activity supports swipe right to delete with Undo and swipe left to edit.

## 2. Dashboard 2.0

- Priority-aware offline insight card routes directly to recurring reviews, budgets, forecast or reports.
- 30-day cash-flow rhythm visual.
- User-controlled Home sections stored in DataStore.
- Existing balance, quick actions, budget health, planning, wealth and recent activity remain intact.

## 3. Planning Centre

- Plan-health score based only on recorded commitments, dates, goal progress and outstanding balances.
- Overdue and due-soon explanations.
- Debt payoff comparison showing Snowball and recorded-APR Avalanche orderings.
- The comparison is explicitly organisational and not financial advice.

## 4. Wealth Centre

- Accessible allocation donut.
- Concentration and diversification notes derived from recorded positive value.
- Existing performance, manual valuation history, liabilities and net-worth statement remain available.

## 5. Analytics Pro

- Money behaviour panel with average daily spending, transaction count, no-spend days, spending variation and income stability.
- Top-payee analysis alongside category, account and daily breakdowns.
- More offline insight rules for no-spend days and volatility.

## 6. Offline smart insights

All insights are calculated on-device from cleared records. No AI API, internet permission, tracking or cloud upload is involved.

## 7. Quality and compatibility

- Package ID remains `com.mohnishraj.goldmineledger`.
- Room remains version 3 with explicit 1→2 and 2→3 migrations.
- Existing backups remain compatible (`ledgerly-backup` internal format identifier is preserved).
- Google OAuth, Drive backup and Supabase remain intentionally deferred.
